Piano Theory

Privacy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Piano Theory has no account system, no analytics and no advertising. It collects no personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not ask for your name, email address or any identifier. There is no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash reporter that carries personal data.

The microphone

If you turn on listening in a lesson, the app uses the microphone to hear which piano key you played. The sound is analysed on your phone, as it arrives, and then discarded. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

What stays on your phone

Your progress through the lessons, your answers to the end-of-lesson question, and any songs you import from files. None of it is transmitted to us. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details and receive no personal information from Apple.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Should we ever begin collecting data, we would say so here and in the app before doing it.
